Privacy Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026

This guide is a free, non-partisan website. It has no accounts, no logins and no database of visitors. This page explains the little information that does leave your browser, and where it goes.

Your address

You only share an address if you type one to find your ballot. It is never stored by this site, never added to the page link, and never sent to analytics. To work out your districts, your browser sends it directly to these public services:

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Photon, an OpenStreetMap address service run by komootThe text you type in the address boxTo suggest matching addresses as you type
U.S. Census Bureau GeocoderThe address you choose, or its map coordinatesTo find your county, town, school and legislative districts
NYC Open Data (City of New York)Map coordinates only, for New York City addressesTo find your City Council district

Each service has its own privacy policy and may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address.

Requesting coverage for your area

If your area isn't covered yet, you can ask us to add it. That form sends only your town or city, your ZIP code and, if you choose to give it, an email address, to a Google Form we manage. We use it only to decide which areas to add next and, if you left an email, to tell you once your area is added. We never ask for your street address there.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't set cookies or use analytics. It remembers nothing about you between visits.

If advertising is added in the future, ads may be provided by Google AdSense, which uses cookies to show and measure ads. This policy will be updated before that happens, and Google's practices are described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Corrections and questions

Every position in this guide links to its primary source. If you spot an error, or have a question about this policy, please report it here.
